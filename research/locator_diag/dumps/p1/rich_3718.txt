CAUSE: The `Panel` title is rendered using `console.render(title_text, ...)` which ignores the `Panel` style (background color).

FIX: Instead of `yield from console.render(title_text, ...)`, use `yield from console.render(title_text, ..., style=style)` to ensure the panel's background style is applied to the title.

REPRO: none
