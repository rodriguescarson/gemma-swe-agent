FILES: rich/text.py:987-1003 (Text.append)
CAUSE: Appending a `Text` instance to itself causes its length to be added to `self._length` and its contents to `self._text` multiple times in a way that can lead to exponential growth if appended repeatedly, although a direct single self-append just doubles it. More importantly, `Text.append` doesn't check for self-reference, which can cause infinite loops in other parts of the system that recursively process `Text` objects or when `Text` is used in a way that expects a finite representation.
FIX: Add a check at the beginning of `Text.append` to prevent appending a `Text` instance to itself.
